# Data sources

The starter records in `index.html` (`seedData()` and `DIRECTORY`) come only from the public sources listed here. Nothing private was invented: contract terms, split sheets, demo submissions, and any phone number or email for an artist or the label are left blank for the team.

Confidence levels:

- **Verified:** read on the source page (or on the official site's own page) in October 2026.
- **Secondary:** taken from a search snippet or a third-party article. Confirm before relying on it.

## Distribution, pitching and rights

- **ONErpm**: https://onerpm.com. Distribution and marketing partner. Its distribution-partners page lists Boomplay (search snippet, not opened). Secondary.
- **Spotify for Artists pitching**: https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/pitching-music-and-videos-to-playlist-editors/. Verified. One song at a time, at least 7 days before release. The next pitch opens once the pitched song goes live. Compilations and songs by featured artists are not pitchable. Dashboard: https://artists.spotify.com.
- **Apple Music for Artists**: https://artists.apple.com. Verified: artist profile, analytics and Shazam data. Editorial pitches go through Apple Music Connect, used by labels and distributors (secondary: Music Ally, 24 Feb 2026; iClarified, 18 Feb 2026).
- **Matchfy.io**: https://matchfy.io. Verified: paid curator and influencer outreach, with prices shown on the site. Treat as a paid service and compare terms.
- **Uganda Performing Right Society (UPRS)**: https://uprs.go.ug. Collecting society for musicians, songwriters, composers and publishers. Official About page (search snippet): Plot 2240, Diplomat Zone, Kansanga Ggaba Road, Kampala. Phone numbers +256 771 952 357 and +256 751 067 197. Email info@uprs.go.ug (UPRS Facebook page, secondary).
- **Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB)**: https://ursb.go.ug. Copyright and neighbouring-rights registration under the Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act, Cap 222. Copyright enquiries: copyright@ursb.go.ug (URSB page). Gazetting is no longer required from 26 September 2025 (secondary: SoftPower, citing a URSB notice of 3 October 2025). The copyright page could not be opened during checking, so confirm current fees and forms on the site.
